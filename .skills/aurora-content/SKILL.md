# Aurora Content & Image Curation Skill

This skill guides all Aurora content writing and image selection to ensure cinematic editorial quality and brand consistency.

## Editorial Copywriting

### Tone of Voice: Luxury Travel
- **English-First**: All UI copy, destination titles, descriptions, and editorial content must be in polished English.
- **Literary & Cinematic**: Writing should feel like a high-end magazine (e.g., Condé Nast Traveler, Aman Journal).
- **Restrained & Premium**: Avoid generic tourism marketing language ("amazing," "must-see," "best").
- **Emotional Geography**: Focus on atmosphere, heritage, and the soul of a place rather than just features.
- **Rhythmic & Clear**: Use short paragraphs with a strong narrative rhythm.

### Grammar & Vocabulary
- Maintain high editorial standards.
- Avoid AI-sounding phrases or "fake luxury" clichés.
- Each destination should feel like a place remembered, not a checklist.

---

## Image Curation

### Selection Rules
- **Match Exact Destination**: Never use mismatched images (e.g., Paris for Ha Long Bay).
- **Emotional Tone**: Images must align with the "Expensive Silence" philosophy.
- **Cinematic Framing**: Prefer wide, editorial compositions.
- **Muted Atmosphere**: Favor early morning, blue hour, or golden hour lighting.
- **Texture & Detail**: Value mist, water, stone, and heritage textures.

### Visual Quality
- **No Over-Saturation**: Avoid the "postcard" or "tourist" look.
- **No Crowds**: Avoid stock-photo style tourist crowds.
- **Authenticity**: Use images that feel like a lived experience, not a staged photoshoot.
- **Next/Image**: Always use `next/image` for performance and optimization.

### Technical Requirements
- **Relevant Alt Text**: Every image must have descriptive, meaningful alt text.
- **No Placeholders**: Never use grey placeholder boxes in final UI.
- **Loading States**: Use designed skeletons only for actual loading states.
- **Broken Images**: Fix or replace broken image links immediately.

## Color & Atmosphere

### Cinematic Palette
Move away from overly bright backgrounds toward a deeper, cooler cinematic palette inspired by the Aurora Hero:
- **Deep Slate** & **Muted Teal**
- **Soft Charcoal** & **Misty Blue-Grey**
- **Restrained Gold** (Accents)
- **Warm Off-White** (Accents/Backgrounds, but not blinding)

### Background Rules
- Avoid pure white backgrounds when sections feel too bright.
- Use soft, muted tones to create depth and focus.

---

## Hero Direction

### Cinematic Montage
The homepage Hero should feel like a seamless cinematic travel montage or atmospheric composite.
- **Locations**: Ha Long Bay, Great Wall of China, Merlion, Mount Fuji.
- **Feel**: Layered, elegant, slow, and premium.
- **Avoid**: Messy collages or AI fantasy clutter.
- **Medium**: Use cinematic still compositions if high-quality footage is unavailable.
